Pokédex is a personal project of mine. I started it because I like Pokémon very much and it helped me to better understand Django. I worked with a sample of the first three generations of Pokémon. All data were drawn from pokeapi.

It's not a flashy web app as others. The colour palette is strict with the colours of each type, the colours of each stat, and white wherever I could not avoid it. The technologies used are Django, Python, HTML, CSS, and Javascript. Search functionality was accomplished with Algolia.
